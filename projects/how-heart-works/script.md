## 1 - The Four-Chambered Muscular Pump
> ANIM: High-fidelity anatomical heart illustration showing division into right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium, and left ventricle; highlighting the one-way tricuspid and mitral valves

The human heart is a precision muscular pump divided into four distinct chambers. Deoxygenated blood returns from the body into the right atrium and passes through the tricuspid valve into the right ventricle. Concurrently, oxygen-rich blood from the lungs fills the left atrium, flowing across the mitral valve into the thick, powerful left ventricle.

## 2 - Pulmonary and Systemic Circulation
> ANIM: Macro visual diagram tracing deoxygenated blue blood to the pulmonary artery and lungs, exchanging gases, and returning as red oxygenated blood pumped through the aorta to the systemic body

The heart drives two continuous circulatory circuits operating in series. In the pulmonary loop, the right ventricle ejects blood through the pulmonary valve toward the lungs to release carbon dioxide and bind oxygen. In the systemic loop, the left ventricle contracts with tremendous force, sending oxygenated blood into the aorta to nourish every vital organ.

## 3 - Electrical Conduction and the Cardiac Cycle
> ANIM: Visual cardiac conduction diagram demonstrating the electrical wave initiating at the SA node, delaying at the AV node, and propagating through the Purkinje fibers to drive synchronized ventricular systole

Every beat is orchestrated by an intrinsic electrical conduction network. The sinoatrial node fires the initial pacemaker impulse across both atria, causing them to contract. The signal pauses at the atrioventricular node to allow the ventricles to fill, then surges down the Purkinje fibers, triggering coordinated ventricular contraction to propel blood forward.
