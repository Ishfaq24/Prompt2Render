## 1 - The Geometry of Right Triangles
> ANIM: A right-angled triangle with legs a and b and hypotenuse c appears with glowing squares on each edge
For every right-angled triangle, an extraordinary geometric relationship connects its three sides. If we construct a square upon each edge, the total area of the two smaller squares matches the area of the largest square on the hypotenuse.

## 2 - The Fundamental Equation
> ANIM: The areas merge together and the equation a squared plus b squared equals c squared is highlighted
This geometric truth gives rise to one of mathematics' most celebrated equations: a squared plus b squared equals c squared. From classical navigation to modern computer graphics, this simple formula underpins how we calculate distance across space.
